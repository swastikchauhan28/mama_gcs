# Phase 76 — Local field coverage preview

Date: 2026-10-27. Baseline: `847d8912af82ef8e87d95dc21abbdb940525ff77`; unrelated `desktop.ini` preserved. User approved tool-width/overlap preview implementation and emulator testing. Hardware work remains on hold.

## Behavior and execution path

Plan route → Coverage → Coverage settings reads the current Field outline. Enter tool working width in metres and overlap percent, choose north/south or east/west, and Generate preview. The offline north-up diagram shows separate working centrelines with alternating directions, ideal tool strips in green and parts outside those strips in pink. It reports field area, total working length excluding turns, and maximum pass spacing. A closer final pass covers the far edge of a rectangular field. No connecting turn is inferred across a concave notch.

`MissionScreen` owns `rememberFieldCoverageState` keyed to outline and draft availability. `FieldCoverageInputs` writes bounded local strings and cancels/clears results on changes. `FieldCoverageState.generate` parses validated values and calls pure `FieldCoveragePlanner.calculate` on `Dispatchers.Default`. Cancellation prevents superseded results from publishing. `FieldCoverageResult` and its Canvas display the metre-plane polygon and bounded pass list. The preview has no repository, telemetry, command, route-export or waypoint-replacement callback. Outline changes reset settings/results; they are volatile and may reset on screen recreation.

## Data, bounds and limitations

- Inputs: current draft/imported outline (untrusted planning coordinates), required width 0.1–50 m, overlap 0–80%; 10% is only an editable initial planning allowance. Each numeric field is capped at 16 characters. No new dependency, permission, schema, external destination or credential handling.
- Geometry: 3–64 vertices, no repeated coordinates or self-crossing/touching nonadjacent edges, at least 1 m², latitude within ±80°, extent at most 2 km. Local equirectangular projection unwraps compact dateline fields. Half-open scanline intersections split concave polygons into separate working segments. At most 500 rows/segments; over-budget outlines fail visibly instead of truncating. Calculation and rendering are bounded by these limits.
- Footprints are ideal rectangular strips with flat ends, visually clipped to the field. Pink is a geometric preview, not measured unploughed soil. No sampled coverage percentage is claimed. Tool overhang is not shown outside the field; UI explicitly requires edge/obstacle review. Small gaps can be hidden by display scale.
- No headland/turn planning, obstacle holes, arbitrary row bearing, tool offset, slope, slip, RTK tracking, surveyed-boundary proof or worked-area tracking. Nominal width/overlap cannot guarantee physical coverage. Nothing uploads or executes a mission.
