# USB-phone Bluetooth Classic receive check — 17 November 2026

Baseline: `9b9b740`, clean `main` worktree. The user requested testing the USB-connected physical phone with the remote through Mama GCS. This was an observation of the app's read-only receive path; no app build/update, saved-data reset, Cube configuration, RC observation, stick movement, mission upload or vehicle command was performed. Device addresses and raw payloads are omitted.

## Observed sequence

1. ADB initially listed no device. Windows showed the phone as an MTP portable device. After the user toggled USB debugging and accepted its prompt, ADB listed the physical phone as authorized; Mama GCS was already installed, open and awake, and Android Bluetooth was on.
2. Android's Connected devices screen listed `T12_485` under previously connected devices. This is a pairing record, not evidence of a live serial session. The operator reported selecting `T12_485` in Mama GCS and opening RC & PWM. No new Android pairing was required.
3. In an early Classic session, Analyze telemetry showed `ACTIVE SESSION · CLASSIC`, 541,352 input bytes, 6,369 decoded supported messages (322 MAVLink 1 / 6,047 MAVLink 2), and subsequently more than 7,000 accepted selected-autopilot messages. Heartbeat timing showed `ACCEPTED HEARTBEAT RECENT`, age 795 ms, zero completed gaps over five seconds and a longest completed interval of 3,083 ms. These are snapshots, not an endurance result. At a later check the app showed Disconnected while in the foreground. No cause or precise transition was captured; avoid attributing it to radio, phone or app without evidence.
4. A later open Classic session was independently labeled `T12_485` in Mama GCS's Classic serial-link card. It showed 2,728 received chunks / 457,702 input bytes; the decoder showed supported MAVLink messages and thousands accepted from the selected autopilot. Across a separate 40-second read-only observation, the top bar remained Connected. The latest visible decoder snapshot included 4,239 accepted selected-autopilot messages, 85 checksum failures and current radio-only status receipt. These totals advanced during observation and are not packet-loss rates.
5. In that later session, the Classic checklist displayed `SOCKET OPEN · WAITING FOR BYTES` and later `MAVLink heartbeat: NOT CONFIRMED` while the same app showed hundreds of thousands of current input bytes. Link & messages showed `ACCEPTED HEARTBEAT RECENT`, age 160 ms, zero completed gaps over five seconds, and longest completed interval 4,006 ms. The checklist and heartbeat-timing panels therefore disagree; no claim about a missing Cube heartbeat should be based on the checklist alone.

## Likely software issue and limits

`ClassicBluetoothScreen` supplies `ClassicConnectionCheck.assess` with a `nowElapsed` value updated once per second by `produceState`, while incoming diagnostic timestamps can advance between ticks. `ClassicConnectionCheck.currentElapsed` rejects any timestamp greater than that cached clock. With nonzero current bytes, this could yield a false `WAITING_BYTES` and suppress later heartbeat checks. This is a code-level hypothesis from the observed discrepancy; reproduce it in a controlled test before changing the freshness logic. Preserve the rule that genuinely future or previous-session timestamps are not accepted. Other session-boundary/state timing causes have not been excluded.

Bluetooth pairing and a socket do not authenticate the remote or vehicle. A CRC-valid MAVLink stream is not command authority or physical-safety evidence. No RC channel movement, SBUS wiring, rover movement or actuator response was verified. The earlier disconnect remains unexplained, and the later 40-second connected display does not prove sustained stability. An app-generated screenshot/UI hierarchy from this phone was used only for local inspection and is not committed. The temporary Android screenshot file was removed after copying to ignored local build evidence.

Next safe step: obtain approval for a scoped software fix/test of the Classic checklist's display clock, then retest the live read-only T12_485 path and record exact session transitions. A separate hardware-team-secured procedure is required for RC and physical behavior tests.
