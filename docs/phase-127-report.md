## Conclusion and next step

The earlier failures were not reproduced in two cold boots and two focused test groups. These observations do not prove that waiting 40 seconds prevents the errors, that memory was the cause, or that the native MapLibre issue is repaired; there was no controlled comparison or repeatable failure to justify a code/configuration change. Use the existing health gate and retain failures if they recur. A future reproduction can collect narrowly scoped emulator and Android logs without exposing raw packets or device identifiers; do not disable Bluetooth, media services, tests or crash checks to obtain a green result. Full software regression, actual physical telemetry and field accuracy, owner review and release gates remain separate and unverified. No commit or push was requested.
