Date: 2026-11-03. Starting revision `5212ff4c07c03b04f6c65943ed7798315c2ef257`, clean `main`. Model/version unavailable. User approved a local comparison of two tool-width and overlap combinations. See D-155-01.

## Behavior and boundaries

Coverage now offers **Compare tool settings**. Options A and B each have a width and overlap; both start from the current preview settings and share the current field, direction and headland. Each uses the existing bounded `FieldCoveragePlanner` independently. Cards show each valid option's inputs, working pass count, length and modeled unplanned-area range, or its own invalid reason. Length excludes turns. The UI warns that ranges describe a model, overlapping ranges do not determine which option has fewer gaps, narrow gaps may be missed, and no option is selected automatically.

The comparison is in-memory only: it does not modify the draft, normal settings, map selection or a vehicle. Both results clear when comparison inputs change; the panel recreates and cancels its work when the shared field or settings change and cancels on disposal. Inputs are limited to 16 characters and validated by the existing planner's width, overlap and headland rules. Each calculation inherits the 64-vertex, 2 km and 500-pass bounds and 128-by-128 area sampling. Canceled CPU-bound planner work may continue until a bounded calculation returns, but cancellation is checked before publishing a result. Large comparisons can take time and two results can be retained until invalidation/disposal.

Physical accuracy, turns, obstacles, actual soil coverage and rover readiness are not established by these models. The existing direction comparison and normal preview remain separate. Updated [practice guide](mission-planning-walkthrough.md) and [security notes](security.md) describe the feature and boundaries. No new dependencies, permission changes, persistence schema, external destination or hardware operation were added.
